Your spec answered my open question

Completion is a per-quest mode, not a global rule. EnumQuestCompletion is NPC or Instant. So a quest can auto-complete at target or sit at full progress waiting to be handed in — the author picks. Ours default to Instant, which is exactly today's behaviour, so no existing quest changes meaning. Complete with … renders only in NPC mode.

What I propose is in this spec, and what is not

In. Many active quests · completed history · category · quest log text + completed text · page controls · completion mode (NPC/Instant) + hand-in · the left-category log screen · quest.noquests · the New Quest toast · the dead MasterPrerequisites gate · /xenoquest commands.
Out — each its own spec. The Availability engine (§3.5) is the big one: 4 dialog checks, 4 quest checks, daytime, 2 faction conditions, 2 scoreboard conditions, min level — and you note it is reused by visibility, marks, item giver, conversation and builder. That is a general conditional-content engine, not quest logic, and it should not be born inside a quest spec. Also out: quest types Item / Location / Area Kill / Manual, repeat rules (EnumQuestRepeat: MC/RL Daily/Weekly), mail, nextQuestid chaining, Random Item reward, and the dialog wheel.
Still unresolved: the Factions tab. Your tab group is Inventory → Factions → Quests, but we have no player-facing faction screen at all — client/npc/faction/ holds only the sync mirror. Quests can ship as the second tab now and Factions slot in between later, or Factions comes first so the group is right from the start.
Our objective types do not line up with theirs. Theirs: Item · Dialog · Kill · Location · Area Kill · Manual. Ours: KILL_MOBS · KILL_PLAYERS · KILL_TYPE · DUMMY_DAMAGE · DUMMY_HITS · TALK_TO_NPC. Their one "Kill" covers our three; our two dummy types are ours alone; their Dialog ties to a specific dialog option where our TALK_TO_NPC only counts the conversation. Worth deciding whether to converge or keep both vocabularies.
