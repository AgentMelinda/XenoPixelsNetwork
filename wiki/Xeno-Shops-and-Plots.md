# XenoPixels shops and plots

Player and staff how-to for `[XPSHOP]` item signs and `[XPLOT]` land signs.

The full guide (syntax, clicks, permissions, `/plot` commands, and failure messages) lives here:

**[docs/xeno-sign-shops-and-plots.md](../docs/xeno-sign-shops-and-plots.md)**

Quick copy-paste:

**Shop (1 stone for 2)**

```
[XPSHOP]
minecraft:stone
1x
2
```

**Plot listing** (corners `x,z` — price on the sign should match `/plot sell`)

```
[XPLOT]
10,-20
40,5
500
```

Right-click buys. Sneak-right-click edits (staff). Price line is a number only (`2`), not `2$`. Needs MMO Econ.
