Thanks to Effekseer (https://effekseer.github.io) and the sample authors (Tktk03 for ToonHit).
hakai_channel, hakai_crumble, hakai_erase, hakai_palm: made for XenoPixels by tools/effekseer/gen_hakai_effects.py
(generated textures; the veil follows the structure of the Effekseer 1.6 sample Aura01).
Replace any <slot>/<slot>.efkefc with your own effect of the same name.
missile_thruster, ship_thruster, sparking_aura, sparking_burst: made for XenoPixels by
tools/effekseer/gen_effects.py (generated textures).
aura, aura2, aura3: made for XenoPixels by tools/effekseer/gen_effects.py (generated textures;
aura3 = spiked silhouette + punch/ki-blast edges + form-coloured sparking).
ki: made for XenoPixels by tools/effekseer/gen_effects.py ki. Its plasma web, cell, hexagon and
normal-map textures are Aura05_T, Aura02_T, ShapePattern01_T and Normal01 from the Effekseer 1.6 samples
(00_Version16), and its lightning and burst textures are Thunder01 and Burst01 from 00_Basic; all CC-0.
Techniques after Laser01-03, Aura01_HDR and Simple_Ring_Shape. The other ki textures are generated.
